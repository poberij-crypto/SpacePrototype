# Basic trading implementation plan

> **For agentic workers:** Use superpowers:executing-plans for inline execution.

**Goal:** Buy, transport and sell three goods between the two existing stations.
**Architecture:** A small TradeData module owns goods, prices and cargo validation. Main owns atomic transactions and persistence; a separate market control owns responsive presentation. Existing mission state gains a free transit with zero reward.
**Tech Stack:** Godot 4.5.1, GDScript, ARM64 Android.
**Spec:** User-approved milestone: basic trade, cargo capacity, different prices at two stations.

## Global constraints

- Preserve package, signing key, auto orientation, legacy saves and existing missions.
- Ten cargo units; ore, electronics and medicine occupy one unit each.
- Fixed buy/sell prices per station, with sell below buy at the same station.
- Transactions only at a docked station without a pending flight; positive integer quantities only.
- Save credits and cargo in one document; roll back a transaction if saving fails.
- Delivery contract reserves two units; refuse launch without space. Free transit pays zero.
- Cargo survives backgrounding, continuation and arrival; destruction loses cargo.

## Review focus

- Capacity and funds boundaries, unknown goods, zero and negative quantities.
- Reopening menus must not permit remote trade during a pending flight.
- Reload on both sides of a transit must preserve goods and station prices.
- No credits from docking a free flight, repeated docking or same-station resale.
- Both orientations must keep prices, quantities, action buttons and warnings readable.
